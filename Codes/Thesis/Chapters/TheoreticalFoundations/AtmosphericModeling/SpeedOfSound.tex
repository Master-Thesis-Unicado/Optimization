The speed of sound $a$ depends on temperature according to:

\begin{equation}
a = \sqrt{\gamma_{air}RT}
\end{equation}

where $\gamma_{air} = 1.4$ is the specific heat ratio for air. This relationship is implemented in the model, which first retrieves the temperature profile from the ISA model before computing the velocity.

For trajectory optimization, the conversion between Mach number $M$ and true airspeed $V$ requires the speed of sound:

\begin{equation}
V = M \cdot a(h)
\end{equation}

This conversion is performed throughout the optimization process when evaluating states defined in terms of Mach number, as the aerodynamic and propulsion models typically operate in the Mach-altitude domain.
