Total aircraft weight comprises three principal components:

\begin{equation}
w_{TO} = w_{OE} + w_{PL} + w_{fuel}
\end{equation}

where $w_{TO}$ is a take off weight, $m_{OE}$ is the operating empty weight, $m_{PL}$ is payload weight, and $m_{fuel}$ is fuel weight.
The operating empty weight further decomposes into airframe structure $w_{airframe}$, propulsion system $w_{propulsion}$, and systems components $w_{systems}$:

\begin{equation}
w_{OE} = w_{airframe} + w_{propulsion} + w_{systems}
\end{equation}



The zero-fuel weight is defined as the sum of the operating empty weight and the payload,
\begin{equation}
w_{ZFW} = w_{OE} + w_{PL}
\end{equation}
It represents the aircraft mass excluding usable fuel.

These weight components are specified in the aircraft configuration module, providing the foundation for initial mass calculations and mass evolution tracking throughout the mission.
