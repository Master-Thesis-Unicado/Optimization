The drag polar relationship characterizes the variation of drag coefficient with lift coefficient. The total drag comprises parasitic drag and induced drag components:

\begin{equation}
C_D = C_{D,0} + C_{D,i}(C_L)
\end{equation}

The induced drag component $C_{D,i}$ depends on lift coefficient, which varies with aircraft mass through the trim condition $C_L = W/(qS)$. As mass decreases during flight due to fuel consumption, this causes a reduction in induced drag.
