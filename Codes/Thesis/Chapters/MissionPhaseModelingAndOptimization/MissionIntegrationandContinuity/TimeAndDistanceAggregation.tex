
Total mission time is the sum of phase durations:

\begin{equation}
t_{total} = t_{climb} + t_{cruise} + t_{descent}
\end{equation}

Similarly, total mission range aggregates the horizontal distance covered in each phase:

\begin{equation}
s_{total} = s_{climb} + s_{cruise} + s_{descent}
\end{equation}

The distance components are computed through integration of true airspeed over time for each phase, using the appropriate velocity profiles and time increments determined from the respective optimization or simulation procedures.
