The initial descent state is defined by the terminal state of the cruise phase: altitude $h_{cruise}$, Mach number $M_{cruise}$, and mass $m_{cruise}$ at the transition point. The terminal condition requires reaching target approach altitude $h_{approach}$ with Mach number within tolerance of the target approach Mach $M_{target}$. The penalty system enforces this constraint during optimization, with additional enforcement through post-processing of the cost-to-go matrix at the final altitude stage.
