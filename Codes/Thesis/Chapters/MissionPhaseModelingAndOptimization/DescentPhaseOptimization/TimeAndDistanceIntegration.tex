Time and distance are computed through post-processing of the optimal state sequence following trajectory extraction. The descent rate at each segment is:

\begin{equation}
\text{Descent Rate}_k = |P_{s,k}| = \left|\frac{(T-D)V}{mg}\right|_k
\end{equation}

Time for each segment follows from:

\begin{equation}
\Delta t_k = \frac{|\Delta h_k|}{|P_{s,k}|}
\end{equation}

where the absolute values account for the negative altitude change and negative specific excess power during descent.

Ground distance during descent is computed similarly to climb, integrating true airspeed over time with the small-angle approximation. Summing segment distances throughout the descent profile yields the cumulative distance, which contributes to total mission range calculations.
