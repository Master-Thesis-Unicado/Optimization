Distance accumulation proceeds through:

\begin{equation}
s(t + \Delta t) = s(t) + V_{TAS} \cdot \Delta t
\end{equation}

where $V_{TAS} = M_{cruise} \cdot a(h_{cruise})$ is the constant true airspeed for level cruise at fixed Mach and altitude. The cumulative cruise distance contributes to total mission range calculations.
