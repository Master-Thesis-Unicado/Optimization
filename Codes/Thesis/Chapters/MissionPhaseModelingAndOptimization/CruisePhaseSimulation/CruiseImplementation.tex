Cruise simulation employs explicit Euler integration with fixed time step $\Delta t$. At each time step, the current mass computes drag, determines required thrust, and calculates fuel consumption.

Mass evolution follows:

\begin{equation}
m(t + \Delta t) = m(t) - \dot{m}(t) \cdot \Delta t
\end{equation}

where the fuel flow rate is:

\begin{equation}
\dot{m}(t) = \text{TSFC}(\delta, M, h) \cdot T(\delta, M, h) \cdot N_{eng}
\end{equation}
