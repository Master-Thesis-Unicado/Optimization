Time and distance integration for cruise follows the same segment-wise approach as climb, adapted for level flight conditions. At each time step, the time increment $\Delta t$ is fixed by the simulation time step size.

Ground distance accumulation proceeds through:

\begin{equation}
\Delta s_k = V_{TAS} \cdot \Delta t = M_{cruise} \cdot a(h_{cruise}) \cdot \Delta t
\end{equation}

where $V_{TAS} = M_{cruise} \cdot a(h_{cruise})$ is the constant true airspeed for level cruise at fixed Mach and altitude. The cumulative cruise distance follows from:

\begin{equation}
s_k = \sum_{i=0}^{k} \Delta s_i = V_{TAS} \cdot t_k
\end{equation}

where $t_k = k \cdot \Delta t$ is the cumulative time. The cumulative cruise distance contributes to total mission range calculations.
