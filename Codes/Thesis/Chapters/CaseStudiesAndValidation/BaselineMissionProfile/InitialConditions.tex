Takeoff mass calculation combines operating empty weight, payload, and initial fuel load: $m_{TO} = m_{OE} + m_{PL} + m_{fuel,0}$. For the baseline mission with maximum fuel capacity, initial mass becomes $m_{TO} = 54,954 + 7,200 + 14,500 = 76,654$ kg.

Initial Mach number is computed from initial velocity and local speed of sound: $M_0 = V_0/a(h_0)$. At sea level with $V_0 = 85$ m/s and standard atmospheric conditions, initial Mach number is approximately $M_0 \approx 0.25$, representing typical climb-out speed for regional jet operations.
