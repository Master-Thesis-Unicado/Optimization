Throttle lever position analysis focuses on the climb phase, where lever position variations have the most significant impact on trajectory optimization. During cruise, throttle lever position continuously decreases as aircraft mass reduces due to fuel consumption, while during descent, throttle lever positions remain at idle settings with no variation. The lever position penalty function therefore primarily affects climb phase optimization, where the algorithm must balance fuel efficiency against thrust requirements for altitude gain and acceleration.

Minimum lever positions demonstrate convergence behavior, with values ranging from 0.78 to 0.83.
