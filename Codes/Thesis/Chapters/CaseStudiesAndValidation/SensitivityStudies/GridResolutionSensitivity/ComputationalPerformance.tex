Computational time increases with grid size product $N_{grid} = K \times I \times L$ due to the three-dimensional state space discretization. Simulation times increase from 4.9 minutes for the coarsest grid ($15 \times 15 \times 15$) to 33.5 minutes for the finest grid ($55 \times 55 \times 55$), representing a 6.84 times increase in computational cost.
