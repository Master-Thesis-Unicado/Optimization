Grid resolution variations assess discretization impact on solution accuracy and computational cost. Systematic analysis evaluates nine grid configurations: $K \times I \times L = 15 \times 15 \times 15$, $20 \times 20 \times 20$, $25 \times 25 \times 25$, $30 \times 30 \times 30$, $35 \times 35 \times 35$, $40 \times 40 \times 40$, $45 \times 45 \times 45$, $50 \times 50 \times 50$, and $55 \times 55 \times 55$, quantifying convergence behavior and identifying optimal resolution for accuracy-computation trade-offs. The analysis varies all three grid dimensions simultaneously ($N_{\text{MACH\_SAMPLES}}$, $N_{\text{ALTITUDE\_STEPS}}$, $N_{\text{LEVER\_SAMPLES}}$) from 15 to 55 in increments of 5, maintaining consistent discretization across Mach number, altitude, and throttle lever position dimensions.
