Center of gravity travel analysis quantifies fuel consumption scenario impacts across grid resolutions. The CG travel metric represents the total longitudinal displacement of the aircraft center of gravity throughout the mission, influenced by fuel tank depletion order and trajectory optimization quality. The analysis employs the proportional fuel consumption scenario, where fuel is depleted from all tanks simultaneously in proportion to their respective capacities throughout the mission. CG travel distances decrease from 0.162 m at grid density 15 to 0.138 m at grid density 55, representing a 14.8\% reduction. The decreasing CG travel with finer grid resolutions reflects more precise trajectory optimization, which affects fuel consumption timing and associated CG evolution patterns.

The observed CG travel variations demonstrate clear convergence behavior, with values decreasing monotonically and stabilizing around 0.138-0.140 m for grid densities above 40. This convergence behavior indicates that grid densities above 40-45 capture the essential CG dynamics with acceptable accuracy, while finer grids provide only marginal improvements at significantly increased computational cost.
