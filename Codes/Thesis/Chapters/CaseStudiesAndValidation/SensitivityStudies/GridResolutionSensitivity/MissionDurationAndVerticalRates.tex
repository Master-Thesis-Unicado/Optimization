Unlike terminal Mach number constraints, no explicit constraints are imposed on mission duration in the optimization formulation. The dynamic programming algorithm selects Mach number and throttle lever position trajectories to minimize fuel consumption, and the resulting mission duration emerges as a consequence of these optimal control selections. This modest variation in mission duration indicates that grid resolution primarily affects fuel efficiency through trajectory optimization rather than mission timing. The increasing mission duration with finer grids reflects the optimizer's enhanced ability to exploit more fuel-efficient trajectory segments, which may require slightly longer flight times to achieve optimal fuel consumption.
