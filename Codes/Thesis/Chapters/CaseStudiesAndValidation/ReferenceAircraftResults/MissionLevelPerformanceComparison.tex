Table~\ref{tab:mission_comparison_summary} presents comprehensive comparison of mission-level performance metrics between the reference and optimized missions. The optimized mission achieves a $10.4\%$ reduction in total fuel consumption ($12,712.3$ kg versus $14,192.1$ kg) while maintaining nearly identical total mission distance ($3,993.0$ km versus $3,999.8$ km). The optimization reduces total mission duration by $6.5\%$ ($294.4$ minutes versus $315.0$ minutes), demonstrating improved efficiency across all performance metrics.

The phase-specific analysis reveals significant improvements in climb and descent phases, with climb fuel consumption reduced by $71.0\%$ ($1,117.4$ kg versus $3,860.0$ kg) and descent fuel consumption reduced by $79.7\%$ ($117.3$ kg versus $579.3$ kg). The cruise phase shows a $17.6\%$ increase in fuel consumption ($11,465.6$ kg versus $9,752.8$ kg), which is offset by a $26.1\%$ increase in cruise distance ($3,757.0$ km versus $2,979.2$ km), resulting in improved overall fuel efficiency. The optimized mission achieves a fuel efficiency of $3.18$ kg/km compared to the reference mission's $3.55$ kg/km, representing a $10.4\%$ improvement.
