Analysis of time-fuel trade-offs reveals relationships between mission duration and fuel consumption. Optimal fuel trajectories prioritize fuel efficiency, potentially requiring longer flight times compared to time-optimal trajectories. The optimal climb duration of $t_{DP} = 1,280$ seconds compares to maximum rate of climb duration of $t_{ROC} = 1,180$ seconds, representing 8.5\% longer climb time for 12.7\% fuel reduction.

The trade-off analysis enables identification of Pareto-optimal solutions balancing competing objectives. Multi-objective optimization extensions could explore the fuel-time frontier, providing trajectory options for different operational priorities.
