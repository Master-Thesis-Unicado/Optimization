This chapter demonstrates the application of the developed mission optimization framework to representative case studies. A reference aircraft configuration and a baseline mission definition are first introduced to fully specify the case-study inputs. Based on these inputs, the optimization framework is applied to generate optimized mission trajectories and associated performance metrics.

The presented results are intended to illustrate the behavior, capabilities, and numerical characteristics of the proposed methodology, including its response to varying mission parameters and optimization strategies. Sensitivity and comparative analyses are used to examine the robustness and consistency of the framework under different assumptions. Formal validation against flight-test data or certified mission analysis tools is outside the scope of this thesis and is identified as future work.
