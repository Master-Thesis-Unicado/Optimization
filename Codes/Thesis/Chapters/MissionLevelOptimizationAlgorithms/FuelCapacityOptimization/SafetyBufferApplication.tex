Following convergence, a safety buffer is applied to the converged minimum fuel capacity to account for operational uncertainties and provide margin for mission execution:

\begin{equation}
F_{optimal} = F_{converged} \times (1 + \beta_{safety})
\end{equation}

where $\beta_{safety}$ is the safety buffer percentage,  set to 5\%. This buffer accounts for factors that may increase actual fuel consumption beyond the nominal mission analysis predictions. 
The buffer percentage is configurable through mission configuration parameters, allowing adjustment based on operational requirements and risk tolerance.
