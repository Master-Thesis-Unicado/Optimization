The cruise distance is adjusted using a damped iterative scheme to prevent oscillation and ensure stable convergence:

\begin{equation}
s_{cruise,k+1} = s_{cruise,k} + \alpha \cdot e_k
\end{equation}

where $\alpha \in (0,1]$ is the damping factor, set to 0.75. The damping factor prevents overshooting by limiting the adjustment magnitude to a fraction of the error.

The iteration continues until the range error falls within tolerance:

\begin{equation}
|e_k| < \epsilon_{range}
\end{equation}

where $\epsilon_{range}$ is set to 3 km for mission range optimization.
