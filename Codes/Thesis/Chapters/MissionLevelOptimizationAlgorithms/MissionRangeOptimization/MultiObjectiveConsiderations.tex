\subsection{Multi-Objective Considerations}

The range optimization procedure focuses solely on matching target range without explicit consideration of fuel consumption. However, since cruise distance directly affects cruise fuel consumption, the range-optimal solution may differ from the fuel-optimal solution for missions with fixed range requirements.

For missions where both range and fuel are important, a multi-objective optimization approach could explore the trade-off between range accuracy and fuel efficiency. However, the current implementation addresses range targeting as a separate objective, recognizing that operational requirements typically prioritize either range accuracy or fuel efficiency as the primary concern.
