The following assumptions underpin the methodology:

\begin{itemize} 
\item The aircraft operates strictly within the standard flight envelope limits. 
\item Fuel consumption follows a quasi-steady flow model; fuel system dynamics and sloshing effects are neglected. 
\item Atmospheric conditions are defined by the International Standard Atmosphere (ISA) with zero wind. 
\item Center of gravity shifts are modeled solely as a function of fuel depletion. 
\item Constraints are approximated as mathematical inequalities within the optimization framework. \end{itemize}

These assumptions enable tractable optimization problem formulation while maintaining sufficient fidelity for concept design applications. The limitations and assumptions should be considered when interpreting results and applying the methodology to specific design scenarios.
