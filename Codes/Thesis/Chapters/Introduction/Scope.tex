
The research focuses on trajectory optimization for fixed-wing aircraft operating in subsonic flight regimes. The methodology addresses complete mission profiles encompassing climb, cruise, and descent phases, with particular emphasis on fuel consumption minimization as the primary optimization objective.

The framework is designed for concept design applications where rapid iteration and evaluation of multiple mission configurations are required. The optimization considers three-dimensional state spaces including altitude, Mach number, and throttle lever position, accounting for dynamic mass evolution throughout mission execution.

The methodology incorporates standard ISA atmospheric models, aerodynamic relationships, and propulsion system characteristics typical of commercial transport aircraft. All type of constraints are explicitly modeled or included as a performance limit within the optimization framework.
