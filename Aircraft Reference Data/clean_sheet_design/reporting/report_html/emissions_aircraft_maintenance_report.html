Aircraft Maintenance Emissions & Energy Demand

Methodology Note

The environmental impact of spare parts is determined by scaling the full production impact(including raw materials and manufacturing) of an original component.
If the primary material recycling is accounted for in the production phase, the credit entry method is therefore assumed here as well.
The scaling factor is the ratio of the component's total maintenance material cost over theaircraft's lifetime to the component's initial recurring cost.
